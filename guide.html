01 · TWO CURRENCIES, ONE ARCADE

The stream runs on two separate currencies, and they never mix:

  • Coins are this channel's Twitch channel points — Twitch hands them out for watching, and you spend them on the channel-point redeems (starting games, adding backlog picks, check-ins, wheel spins…).
  • Tokens are the channel's own arcade currency. You earn them by being here and by winning things, and you spend them in chat with commands like !spend, !powerup and !duel. !tokens shows your balance any time.

02 · EARNING TOKENS

You must be a follower to earn — following is the whole entry fee. Subscribers earn 2× on everything below except check-ins.

  • Just being here: 2 Tokens every 2 minutes while the stream is live. No command, it just happens (lurking counts — say !lurk on your way to the couch).
  • Check-In (a Coin redeem): pays 50 × your streak bonus. The streak grows +5% per stream day and caps at 1.5× — miss a stream and it resets, so protect that streak. The first three check-ins of the night get a podium bonus: +30 / +20 / +10.
  • Winning the games — see the chat games: trivia pays 25, Name That Game pays 150 / 75 for 1st / 2nd, Price Is Right pays 200 (+100 for a near-exact price).
  • Winning duels — take the other player's wager, up to 500 a pop.

04 · THE CHAT GAMES

All three run right on the stream overlay. Price Is Right and Name That Game are started by Coin redeems (200 each), so chat decides when a round happens.

🏷️ Price Is Right — 200 Coins

  1. Redeem it and give the stream a moment — it prices a real copy of a game (usually the one on screen first), which can take ~40 seconds.
  2. The cart art and question appear: you have 60 seconds to guess with !guess 24.99 — with or without the $, both are fine. Your first guess is the one that counts — a second one is ignored, so think before you type.
  3. Closest without going over wins 200 Tokens — land within $1.00 of the real price and you get a +100 bonus.

🕹️ Name That Game — 200 Coins

  1. A blurred box cover appears and sharpens over 75 seconds; the round runs 90 seconds total.
  2. Guess in plain chat — no command, just type the game's name.
  3. First correct answer takes 150, second takes 75.
Winners are announced at the reveal, not mid-round. Nothing confirms a correct guess while the round runs — so copying someone else's answer is just a gamble on a stranger's spelling. Titles with a subtitle need both halves: “star wars” alone will not take Star Wars Episode I: Jedi Power Battles — give it a couple of words from each side.

🧠 Trivia — free

Trivia fires during ad breaks (so there is always something to do): a question about a random game from the library appears on the lower third with a 60-second countdown. Answer in plain chat — every correct answer inside the window earns 25 Tokens.

05 · DUELS

Challenge anyone in chat to a straight 50/50 wager:

  1. !duel <user> <amount> — wagers run 10–500 Tokens.
  2. They have 60 seconds to answer with !duelaccept (or chicken out with !dueldecline).
  3. The winner takes the whole pot — and the corner of the stream plays the duel out: both avatars clash, and the winner literally slices the loser's card in half.

06 · THE GAME QUEUE

The queue on the right side of the stream is the list of games chat has asked RetroSai to play, in order.

  • Getting a game in: redeem Game Queue Request with Coins and type the game you want (there is a per-person cooldown — !qcd shows yours).
  • Checking it: !queue (or !q) reads the line-up out in chat; the overlay shows it live, and the live queue page mirrors it on the web even when you're not watching.
  • Jumping the line: !spend queueskip (600 Tokens) moves your latest request to the front — marked with a token badge.
  • The 888 VIP: !888 rolls 1–888. Hit exactly 888 and you're the reigning VIP — your requests outrank everything, marked with the pink diamond, until someone dethrones you.
  • When your game gets loaded, the stream title says “Requested by @you” — your moment in the marquee.

07 · THE BACKLOG — GETTING A GAME ON THE PILE

The backlog is chat's wishlist of games for future streams — stuff that deserves a proper session. The most-voted games float to the top, and the whole list lives at the public backlog page.

Two ways to vote

  1. The redeem: spend 500 Coins on Add Game to Backlog and type the game's name. If it's already on the pile, your redeem stacks a vote on it instead — and if the name can't be recognised at all, your Coins are refunded.
  2. Tokens: !spend backlog <game> — 75 Tokens per vote, and you can stack multiple at once with x2…x10 on the end.

Good to know

  • !backlogs prints the current top 5 in chat.
  • Typo'd the name? !backlogedit <correct game> re-points your most recent vote — free within 15 minutes.
  • Votes are counted per game, so rallying chat behind one title is exactly the strategy it sounds like.
  • When a backlog game gets played (or gets a hard pass) it's retired from the board — the page keeps the history.

08 · HIGH SCORES & THE MONTHLY PRIZE

  • !tokenboard (!tlb) — the seasonal HIGH SCORES board: top Token earners, arcade style. It also slides the board onto the stream.
  • !alltime (!atb) — the all-time earners board.
  • The prize: each month's top earner wins a $10 gift card (delivered by whisper — or a gift sub if the winner isn't subbed). September 2026 is season one: it counts everything since launch, then October starts fresh.

09 · YOUR GAME SHELF

Every game that gets played while you're in chat goes on your shelf — a permanent, public collection of everything you've witnessed here. Browse everyone's shelves, or jump straight to yours at shelf.html?u=yourname (chat shortcut: !shelf). Shelf pages show your Twitch profile picture, your collection count, and every box, spine out.
